# Sales-copy principles (Dorset Rewires)

Apply these to every customer-facing / marketing / sales page, ALONGSIDE the ADHD-copy
rules (short, active, concrete, scannable). Two lenses: Alex Hormozi (make the offer a
no-brainer) and Alex Becker (make them actually read it).

## Hormozi - make the offer irresistible
- Value equation: (Dream outcome x Likelihood it works) / (Time delay x Effort). Maximise
  the top, minimise the bottom. Show the result is big, believable, fast, and easy.
- Stack and NAME the value out loud. Do not imply it - list what they get and what it is
  worth in pounds.
- Risk reversal / guarantees. Remove the fear. E.g. "Fixed price - it only changes if you
  change the job." "Costs you nothing."
- Specificity beats vagueness. "£300" and "£265" beat "great commission" and "a free check".
- Remove effort and friction. Every extra step or field loses people. "Takes ten seconds."
- The no-brainer test: is the value so high and the risk so low they would feel stupid
  saying no?

## Becker - make them read it
- A punchy eyebrow / pattern-interrupt above the headline. Stop the scroll.
  E.g. "£300 for a text message."
- Lead with the reader's self-interest, bluntly. What is in it for THEM, in the first line.
- Cut fluff and soft closes. "Everyone wins" is limp - sharpen it to self-interest.
- Short. Punchy. Confident. Fewer words, more hits.
- Urgency / scarcity where it is honest (limited capacity, few partners per area).

## Dorset Rewires specifics
- Brand promise: "Quotes, not estimates - the price we quote is the price you pay."
- Perk by job type: a free EICR (renovations / extensions) OR a free temporary power board
  for the builders (full rewire / new build).
- Keep everything ADHD-scannable (see the ADHD-copy hook) and follow naming-convention.md
  for any code identifiers.

When you write or edit a DR sales page, run the draft past BOTH lenses before saving.
