Why Bournemouth homes need rewiring

Much of Bournemouth’s housing stock is pre-1980 and still on its original wiring - the 1930s semis along the Wallisdown corridor, the Edwardian terraces in Boscombe, the post-war estates in Kinson and Throop. Old rubber and early PVC insulation past its safe service life is a common find.
