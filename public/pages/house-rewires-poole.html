Poole-specific rewire considerations

Poole’s housing mix swings hard between waterfront new-build and pre-war semis inland. Branksome Park has a high proportion of 1920s-1930s detached homes; Parkstone is mostly Edwardian terraces; Sandbanks and the Harbour fringe have significant 1960s-70s coastal builds with corroded steel-conduit and lead-sheathed origins. We adapt the rewire scope accordingly.
