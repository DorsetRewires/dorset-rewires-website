How your home gets wired, stage by stage

Every self-build follows the same clear path, all to the current wiring regulations. Here is exactly what happens and when.

  1. Planning and design. We get involved early, from the design stage. We help you set out every circuit to your drawings - where the sockets, lights, data points and EV charger really want to go - so first fix runs to a clear plan, with nothing missed while the walls are open.
  2. First fix. This is the big one, and it happens before the plasterers arrive. We run all the cabling and fit the back boxes while the walls and floors are open. Book us in ahead of plastering - this stage sets your programme.
  3. Second fix. Once the walls are done, we fit the consumer unit (the fuse board), all the sockets, switches, data points and lights, then fit off and make live.
  4. Testing and inspection. We test and inspect the whole installation to BS7671, the 18th Edition wiring regulations, so every circuit is proven safe before you move in.
  5. Certificate and handover. You get an Electrical Installation Certificate for your home, and we notify Building Control (LABC) for the notifiable work. Your home is ready.
