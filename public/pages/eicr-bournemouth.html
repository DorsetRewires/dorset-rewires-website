What an EICR is

An EICR (Electrical Installation Condition Report) is a thorough check of the fixed wiring in a property - the consumer unit, the circuits, the sockets, the earthing and bonding. We test it, then write a report that says whether the installation is satisfactory or not, and lists anything that needs putting right. It is the electrical equivalent of an MOT.

Who needs one

  • Landlords - it is the law. Every rented home in England needs a valid EICR, renewed at least every 5 years
  • Buyers and sellers - peace of mind before money changes hands
  • Homeowners - if your home is 10 years past its last check, or you have just moved in
  • Anyone planning work - it tells you whether you need a new consumer unit, a rewire, or nothing at all

What the codes mean

  • C1 - danger present. A risk of injury. We make it safe straight away
  • C2 - potentially dangerous. Needs fixing soon. A C2 makes the report unsatisfactory
  • C3 - improvement recommended. Not a fail, just a sensible upgrade
  • FI - further investigation needed to be sure

We explain every code in plain English, so you know exactly what matters and what does not.

What happens after

If the report is satisfactory, you get your certificate and you are done. If something needs putting right, we quote the remedial work in writing - no pressure, and you are free to use anyone you like. If we do the remedials, the re-test is just £85.

Quick answers

How much is an EICR in Bournemouth?

It is a fixed price by how many circuits you have: £140 for a 1-bed flat, £195 for a 2-3 bed, £265 for a 4-5 bed, and £345 for a large home or HMO. There is no VAT to add.

Do landlords legally need an EICR?

Yes. Private landlords in England must have a valid EICR for each rented property, renewed at least every 5 years, and give the tenant a copy.

How long does the report take?

We send the full PDF report within 24 hours of the inspection.

What if it fails?

We quote any remedial work in writing, with no pressure to use us. If we do the work, the re-test is just £85.
