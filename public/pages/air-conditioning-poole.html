Why Poole homes are buying air conditioning

Poole's housing mix is made for this product. South-facing flats along the harbour, at Lilliput and Sandbanks, that overheat from spring onwards. Conservatories on the 1930s semis of Branksome and the family homes of Canford Heath and Creekmoor that are abandoned every August. Garden offices from Upton to Merley where working from home means sweating from home. And plenty of homes with no gas supply paying heavily for electric heating every winter.

One wall-split unit answers both seasons: cooling in the heatwaves, and winter heating at roughly a third of the running cost of plug-in heaters - because every unit we fit is a reversible air-to-air heat pump. Dorset's mild coastal winters are exactly where that heating efficiency peaks.

What we fit

  • Harbour and clifftop flats - quiet units, sensible siting, freeholder and planning checks done at the survey
  • Bedrooms and living rooms - single splits fitted in a day
  • Conservatories and garden rooms - including the power supply to the building where needed
  • Multi-room systems - one outdoor unit, up to four rooms

How the install works

Every install is a two-trade job. Our electricians handle the survey, mounting, pipework routing, condensate, wiring and testing - the half most air conditioning firms subcontract out. The refrigerant connections, pressure test and commissioning are completed by an F-Gas certified engineer, and the manufacturer's warranty is registered on completion. One firm, one boss: Pete quotes the job, runs the job and stays your single point of contact.

What it costs

Nationally, most homeowners pay between £1,600 and £2,800 for a single-room wall split supplied and installed, and £3,500 to £7,000 for multi-room systems. Quick answers

How much does air conditioning installation cost in Poole?

Nationally, most homeowners pay between £1,600 and £2,800 for a single-room wall split, supplied and installed. We quote every Poole job fixed, in writing, after a free survey, with no VAT to add.

Is a coastal location a problem for the outdoor unit?

Salt air is harder on outdoor kit, which matters along the harbour and at Sandbanks. We site the condenser sensibly, use coastal-appropriate fixings, and the manufacturer's warranty is registered on every unit at commissioning.

Can you do my garden room or home office?

Yes - garden offices are one of the most common installs. A small wall split makes the room usable in August and warm in January, and we handle the power supply to the building as well if it needs one. That part is our own trade.

Who does the refrigerant work?

The refrigerant connections, pressure test and commissioning are completed by an F-Gas certified engineer, as the law requires. Our electricians do the survey, mounting, pipework, wiring and testing, and Pete stays your single point of contact.

Do you cover my part of Poole?

Yes - all the BH12 to BH17 postcodes, including Parkstone, Branksome, Canford Heath, Creekmoor, Hamworthy, Oakdale and Broadstone next door. Pete is based in Parkstone, so Poole is home turf.
