Why Bournemouth homes need an electrician

Bournemouth is a mix of grand Victorian and Edwardian houses, 1930s semis, and a lot of flats - many of them old conversions of big period homes near the centre and in Boscombe. Older properties often still have their original wiring and a fusebox with no RCD protection. Flats and houses in multiple occupation (HMOs), which Bournemouth has plenty of around the centre and the university, legally need a current EICR.

The seafront adds salt air, which corrodes outdoor sockets and lights faster than inland, so we fit weatherproof gear where it is needed. If you are not sure whether you need anything, we will do a free survey and a quick on-the-spot check.
