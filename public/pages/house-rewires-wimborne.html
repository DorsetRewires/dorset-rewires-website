Wimborne's homes, and why they get rewired

Wimborne is a town of older houses. Cottages and town houses around the Minster, Victorian and Edwardian streets off the centre, 1930s semis towards Colehill, and the later estates out at Merley. Beautiful stock - and a lot of it still running on wiring from a different era.

  • Pre-1960s wiring - rubber-insulated cable goes brittle with age and crumbles when touched. If your house has never been rewired, this is the one to check
  • Old fuse boxes - a board with rewirable fuses or a wooden back has none of the shock protection a modern board gives you
  • Renovation projects - buying a period place to do up? Rewiring while walls are open is far cheaper than after the decorating is done
  • Extensions built on old circuits - decades of add-ons can leave a house with overloaded circuits nobody planned

Signs the wiring is due

  • Fuses blow or trips happen more than rarely
  • Sockets are warm, crackle, or smell of hot plastic
  • Lights flicker when appliances switch on
  • Two-pin sockets or fabric-covered cable anywhere in the house
  • An electrician has ever told you "this board needs looking at" - and nobody looked

Not sure? An electrical safety report settles it with evidence before you commit to anything.

Period and listed properties

Around the Minster and the conservation streets, houses deserve a careful hand. Conservation rules mostly govern the outside of a building, so the rewire itself is rarely restricted - but a listed building can need consent for some internal work, and we tell you before anything starts, not after. In period rooms we plan cable routes to spare original plaster and joinery, and we leave surfaces ready for the decorator.

Quick answers

How much does a rewire cost in Wimborne?

It goes by bedroom count: a 1-bed home runs £2,800 to £4,000, a 2-bed £3,800 to £5,500, a 3-bed £4,500 to £7,000, a 4-bed £6,500 to £11,000, and a 5-bed or bigger £10,000 to £16,000. Can we live in the house during the rewire?

Most families do. We work room by room, keep the dust contained, and get power and lighting back on for the evening wherever the job allows. It is disruptive - we will not pretend otherwise - but you rarely need to move out.

Does a rewire need Building Control sign-off?

Yes - a full rewire is notifiable work under Part P of the Building Regulations. You get the certificates that prove the work was done and checked properly, which matter when you sell or remortgage.

My house is old - near the Minster. Does that change anything?

Usually not the wiring itself. Conservation rules mostly cover the outside of a building. A LISTED building is different - some internal work can need consent, and we flag that before anything starts. Either way we route cables with care in period rooms and put things back the way we found them.
