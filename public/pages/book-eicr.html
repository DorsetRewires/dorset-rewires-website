Book your EICR online

Your electrical safety report, booked without a phone call. Pay the fixed price for your property size, then pick a time that suits you. Done. If no date works for you, we refund in full - no questions.

How it works

  1. Choose your property size below and pay. The price is fixed by circuit count and there is no VAT on top
  2. Pick your appointment. Payment takes you straight to a live calendar - choose any open slot from 48 hours ahead
  3. We turn up and test. Your full PDF report lands in your inbox within 24 hours of the inspection

After you pay

  • The calendar opens automatically. Slots run evenings and weekends as well as weekdays, from 48 hours ahead
  • You get an email confirming the time - and a reminder before the day
  • Nothing suits? Tell us and we refund the full amount straight back to your card. Paying first should never cost you flexibility

What your money buys

  • A full inspection and instrument test of the fixed wiring to BS7671 - board, circuits, sockets, earthing
  • The complete PDF report within 24 hours, every observation coded and photographed
  • Any live danger made safe before we leave, included in the price - never a second call-out fee
  • A local electrician on the end of the phone - the person who tests your property is the person you booked
