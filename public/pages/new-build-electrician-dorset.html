New Build Electrical Contractor - Dorset Developers & Self-Builders

We wire your plots from first-fix to final safety certificate. Conversions and self-builds too. We handle the paperwork (NHBC sign-off, Building Control, Premier Guarantee). 2.5% retention accepted as standard.

Why developers use us

  • Programme reliability: dates locked as soon as you are ready to give the go-ahead, penalty terms accepted
  • Capacity: single plots through to multi-plot schemes
  • Single point of contact: Pete, the owner, on every job
  • NICEIC self-certification: EICs issued per plot, same day
  • Retention-friendly: 2.5% for 12 months accepted as standard
  • Spec range: standard 18th Edition through to high-spec residential (CAT6 backbone, EV-ready charge points, PV-ready supplies, MVHR-integrated)
