New Build Electrical Contractor - Dorset

We wire your plots from first fix to final safety certificate. Developers, builders and self-builders across Dorset. Conversions too. We handle the paperwork - Building Control, Premier Guarantee. We accept 2.5% retention as standard.

Why developers use us

  • Programme reliability: dates locked as soon as you are ready to give the go-ahead, and we accept penalty terms
  • Capacity: single plots through to multi-plot schemes
  • One point of contact: Pete, the owner, on every job
  • An employed team: the work is done by Pete and our own PAYE electricians, all under Pete's supervision - no dodgy subcontractors
  • Certification: an Electrical Installation Certificate issued per plot
  • Retention-friendly: 2.5% for 12 months accepted as standard

What we cover

The whole electrical package, on new builds and conversions:

  • First fix - cabling, back boxes and containment to the plot drawings
  • Second fix - boards, accessories, lighting and testing
  • Final certification - EIC per plot, ready for handover
  • Self-builds and one-off homes - the same service for a single dream home

Spec range

From a standard 18th Edition installation through to high-spec residential:

  • CAT6 data backbone for a wired-in smart home
  • EV-ready charge points on the drive
  • PV-ready supplies for solar
  • MVHR-integrated wiring for airtight, low-energy builds

Certification and paperwork

You get an Electrical Installation Certificate per plot, and we notify Building Control for notifiable work. We are familiar with NHBC, LABC and Premier Guarantee sign-offs.

Trade references

Available on request. We will put you in touch with a recent past client whose project is closest in size and spec to yours, so you can hear about the work and our reliability first-hand.

Quick answers

Do you take on multi-plot developments?

Yes. We wire single plots through to multi-plot schemes, from first fix to final certificate, with one point of contact on every job.

Do you accept retention?

Yes. We accept JCT-style 2.5% retention released 12 months after practical completion, agreed in writing at quote stage.

Who handles certification and Building Control?

We do. We issue an Electrical Installation Certificate per plot and notify Building Control.

How do I get a price for a scheme?

Email Pete the spec and programme. We send a tendered response within 5 working days, with a capability pack on request.
